03 - REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION

This document outlines the functional and non-functional requirements of the system as identified through reverse engineering of the implementation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS (RF)

ACADEMIC MANAGEMENT
- RF-001: Register and manage students with personal and B2B data.
- RF-002: Define training programs (Courses) with specific approval criteria (Grades and Attendance).
- RF-003: Create training classes (Turmas) with scheduling and instructor allocation.
- RF-004: Automate enrollment management, including price calculation and status tracking.
- RF-005: Record student attendance and lesson topics.
- RF-006: Record student grades and automatically calculate final averages.
- RF-007: Generate and issue digital certificates upon course completion.
- RF-008: Support bulk certificate issuance for entire classes.

HUMAN RESOURCES (RH)
- RF-009: Manage employees with comprehensive legal and financial metadata.
- RF-010: Track employee contracts, including types and salary components.
- RF-011: Recording and managing employee timesheets (Clocking).
- RF-012: Process monthly payroll with automated tax calculations (IRT/INSS).
- RF-013: Manage leave requests (Ferias) and absences.
- RF-014: Register salary advances and deductions with status-based approval flows.
- RF-015: Generate payslip documents (Recibos de Vencimento) in multiple formats (PDF, XLSX).

FINANCIAL & AUDIT
- RF-016: Register payments against student enrollments.
- RF-017: Provide real-time dashboards for revenue and pending balances.
- RF-018: Maintain a non-volatile audit log for all sensitive data mutations.
- RF-019: System-wide configuration for company identity and fiscal constants.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS (RNF)

SECURITY & ACCESS
- RNF-001: Authentication must be performed via secure session management (NextAuth).
- RNF-002: Access control must be granular, allowing permissions at Module and Item levels.
- RNF-003: Sensitive data (passwords) must be hashed.

PERFORMANCE & SCALABILITY
- RNF-004: Critical operations (bulk certificates, payroll) must utilize database transactions for atomicity.
- RNF-005: Asynchronous task processing (Email Worker) must handle high-volume communications without blocking UI.
- RNF-006: Search operations must be optimized utilizing appropriate indexing in the schema.

DATA INTEGRITY & COMPLIANCE
- RNF-007: Decimal types must be used for all financial calculations to prevent floating-point errors.
- RNF-008: All identifying numbers (BI, NIF, INSS) must have uniqueness constraints.
- RNF-009: Historical records (Contracts, Payroll) must be immutable once processed or ended.

USABILITY & INTERNATIONALIZATION
- RNF-010: Provide multi-language support (Portuguese/English).
- RNF-011: Support both Dark and Light themes.
- RNF-012: Layout must be responsive for administrative use on various devices.
